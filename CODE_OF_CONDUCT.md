# Code of Conduct

We want MyAudioBook to be a friendly and useful place for everyone who takes part in it, in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good faith, and ask questions before drawing conclusions.
- Give feedback that is specific and kind; accept feedback gracefully.
- Respect other people's privacy. Never share personal data or private recordings without permission.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Spam, trolling, or deliberately disrupting discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through a GitHub Security Advisory draft or by the contact details on the maintainers' GitHub profiles. Reports are treated confidentially.
